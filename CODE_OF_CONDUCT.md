# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Cloud Vault a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Being kind, respectful and constructive
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users
- Accepting graceful feedback and giving the same in return
- Taking responsibility, apologizing, and learning from mistakes

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualized language or imagery, unwelcome advances
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers via a private GitHub advisory
or by opening a confidential contact through the repository owner's profile.
All complaints are reviewed promptly and treated fairly; the reporter's safety
and privacy are prioritized.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith
may face consequences determined by the other maintainers, including removal
from the project.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests,
discussions, chats) and in public spaces when an individual is representing
the project.
